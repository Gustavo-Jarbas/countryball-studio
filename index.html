HOW IT WORKS

The creator stays in control from connection to publication.

Every consequential step is visible and requires a deliberate user action.

01

Connect securely

The creator authorizes their own account through TikTok Login Kit. Credentials are never entered into Countryball Studio.

02

Review the final video

The exact MP4 is previewed before upload. The creator can edit the caption and review all posting options.

03

Choose settings

Privacy, comments, Duet, Stitch, commercial disclosure and AI-generated-content settings remain under creator control.

04

Approve and track

Upload begins only after explicit consent. Transfer and TikTok processing status remain visible until completion or failure.

BUILT FOR ORIGINAL CREATORS

A publishing workspace, not an invisible autoposter.

Countryball Studio is designed for creators who produce their own educational animations. It organizes completed videos, prevents accidental duplicate submissions, and keeps the final publishing decision with the person who owns the content and the connected account.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Production files and authorization data stay on the creator's computer.

The public website has no account system, analytics or advertising trackers. In the desktop application, videos remain local until the creator explicitly authorizes a transfer to TikTok. OAuth tokens and local publication records are kept on the user's device and can be removed by disconnecting the account.
