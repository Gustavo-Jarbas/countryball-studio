Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Countryball Studio processes information through its public website and local desktop publishing workflow. Countryball Studio is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

2. Local-first design

The desktop application runs on the creator's own computer. Project files, rendered videos, captions, posting preferences, OAuth tokens and publication records are stored locally by default. Countryball Studio does not operate a central account database for this workflow.

3. Information processed when you connect TikTok

When you choose to connect a TikTok account, TikTok Login Kit handles authentication. Subject to the permissions you approve, the application may receive and locally store a TikTok account identifier, display name, profile image URL, access token, refresh token, granted scopes and token-expiration information. Your TikTok password is never requested, received or stored by Countryball Studio.

4. Video and publishing information

The application processes the local MP4 you select, its caption, hashtags, privacy selection, interaction settings, commercial-content declarations, AI-generated-content setting, scheduled local time and technical upload status. Media and publishing information are sent to TikTok only after you review the post and expressly authorize the upload.

5. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested functions: identify the connected creator account, show account-specific posting options, validate video compatibility, transfer an approved file, monitor processing status, avoid accidental duplicate submissions, refresh authorization and display local publication history.

6. When information is shared

Countryball Studio does not sell personal information and does not share it for advertising. When you approve a post, the selected media and settings are transmitted to TikTok through its official APIs. TikTok processes that information under its own Privacy Policy and terms. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the Service.

7. Public website data

This website does not use an account system, advertising cookies, behavioral analytics or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process standard connection and security logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and publication records remain on the user's computer until they expire, are replaced, are disconnected or are deleted with the local application data. Videos remain wherever the creator stores them. TikTok retains uploaded content and related information according to the creator's TikTok settings and TikTok's policies.

9. Disconnecting and deleting local data

Use the TikTok section of Countryball Studio and select “Disconnect account.” The application requests token revocation and removes its local authorization record. You may also delete local publication records and project files from your computer. You can independently revoke connected-app access from your TikTok account settings. Detailed instructions are available on the Support page.

10. Security

Countryball Studio uses TikTok's OAuth authorization flow, anti-forgery state validation and PKCE for desktop login. Client secrets are not placed in public website code. Because local files are protected by the security of the user's computer, users should maintain operating-system updates, device access controls and secure backups.

11. Your choices and rights

You control whether to connect an account, which scopes to approve, which video to select, what metadata to send and whether to begin an upload. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information. Most Countryball Studio data is local and can be inspected or removed directly by the user. TikTok-specific requests should also be directed to TikTok.

12. Children's privacy

Countryball Studio is intended for creators who are legally able to operate the connected accounts and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through this website.

13. International processing

TikTok and GitHub may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies. Countryball Studio's desktop records remain on the user's device unless the user transfers or backs them up elsewhere.

14. Policy updates

We may update this Policy when the Service, applicable law or third-party platform requirements change. Material revisions will be posted here with a new effective date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or assistance removing local data, use the Support page or contact the developer through the official GitHub profile. Do not post access tokens, passwords or other sensitive information in a public issue.
